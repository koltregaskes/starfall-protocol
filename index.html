World frame

The network died. The ruins kept talking.

Starfall Protocol imagines a post-collapse solar frontier where transit rings, sky docks, and climate engines still drift in half-working chains above inhabited worlds. Your team is one of the few groups capable of getting in and out with both data and survivors.
